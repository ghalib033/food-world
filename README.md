# 🍔 Food World – Online Food Delivery Application

Food World is an online food delivery application designed to make ordering delicious vegetarian and non-vegetarian food simple and convenient. Users can explore food items, manage their cart, place orders using Cash on Delivery (COD), and track their orders.

## ✨ Features

- 🔐 **User Authentication** – User registration and login.
- 🍽️ **Food Categories** – Browse vegetarian and non-vegetarian food.
- 🔍 **Food Menu** – Explore available food items, images, and prices.
- 🛒 **Shopping Cart** – Add items, update quantities, and remove products.
- 📍 **Delivery Address** – Enter customer details and delivery location.
- 🧾 **Checkout System** – Review items, quantities, and total amount before ordering.
- 💵 **Cash on Delivery** – Place orders using the COD payment option.
- ✅ **Order Confirmation** – View order details after successful placement.
- 📦 **My Orders** – View order history and order status.
- ❌ **Order Cancellation** – Support for cancellation with a reason, when implemented.
- 🛠️ **Admin Panel** – Manage food products and orders, according to the implemented features.
- 📱 **Responsive UI** – A user-friendly interface with Food World's red/pink theme.

## 🛍️ How It Works

1. Register or log in.
2. Browse the food menu and choose food items.
3. Add items to the cart and adjust quantities.
4. Proceed to checkout and review customer and delivery details.
5. Confirm the order using Cash on Delivery.
6. View the order confirmation and check order status in My Orders.

## 🔒 Security

- Validate customer input on the backend.
- Verify authenticated users before accessing their orders.
- Calculate prices on the server using trusted product data.
- Prevent duplicate order submissions.
- Keep secrets and credentials out of the repository.
